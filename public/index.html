Projection method

Every available player-season contributes once. Each earlier season receives 0.6 times the weight of the next season; rates also reflect PA or IP, and projected playing time reflects games played. Missing player-seasons are omitted. IP rounds to the nearest half inning; derived stats use projected counts. These are estimates without opponent adjustments.

    HOW RANGE STATS ARE COUNTED

    Only the written play events in the selected games are counted. Hitting AVG = hits / at bats; OBP = (hits + walks + hit by pitches) / plate appearances; SLG = total bases / at bats; OPS = OBP + SLG. Pitching HA, HR allowed, walks, strikeouts and outs come from those same events. PCBL has two outs per inning, so logged IP = logged outs / 2 and logged WHIP = (hits allowed + walks allowed) / logged IP. Earned runs and ERA cannot be safely allocated to individual pitchers from every game log, so they are intentionally omitted. Official season totals remain on the player pages and sheet.
